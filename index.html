About Priyanshu Cafe

A place to slow down and stay awhile.

Priyanshu Cafe brings the warmth of an Italian kitchen to the heart of the city, where every visit feels personal and every table has a story.

We prepare our food with fresh ingredients, handmade pasta, rich sauces, and the kind of patience that turns a simple meal into a memorable evening. From the first cup of coffee to the last bite of dessert, our team is here to make you feel at home.

Come in for a quiet morning, meet friends over lunch, or join us for a long dinner filled with good food and even better conversation.

“Good food brings people together. At Priyanshu Cafe, that is where every beautiful moment begins.”
